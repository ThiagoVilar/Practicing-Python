# Conditionals: if, elif and else

Exercises from the course [Praticando Python: condicionais if, elif e else](https://cursos.alura.com.br/course/praticando-python-condicionais-if-elif-else).

## What I practiced

- Using `if`, `elif` and `else`
- Writing conditionals in practice

## Activities

- [01 - Monitoring sales](./01_monitoring_sales.py): compares apples and bananas sold and shows which had higher sales
- [02 - Calculating total project time](./02_calculating_total_project_time.py): adds the days of three activities and validates that none is negative
- [03 - Server temperature](./03_server_temperature.py): shows an alert if the temperature is above the limit
- [04 - Calculating BMI](./04_calculating_bmi.py): calculates the BMI and shows the weight category
- [05 - Controlling monthly budget](./05_controlling_monthly_budget.py): shows a warning if the monthly expenses exceed the budget limit
- [06 - Office access control](./06_office_access_control.py): allows access only during office hours
- [07 - Classifying students by average](./07_classifying_students_by_average.py): calculates the average of three grades and shows if the student passed, is in recovery or failed
- [08 - Calculating toll](./08_calculating_toll.py): shows the toll value based on the distance traveled
- [09 - Checking number parity](./09_checking_number_parity.py): checks whether a number is even or odd
- [10 - Approving loan](./10_approving_loan.py): approves a loan based on the monthly income and the desired installment
